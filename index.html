      A living pixel-art pond

      Procedural Pond is a self-running aquarium in your browser. Every animal is a chain of joints that follows its head, the idea from argonautcode's procedural animation sketch, drawn by a tiny voxel renderer into chunky, shaded pixel art.

      What happens here

      • Koi, tetras, eels, axolotls, turtles, crabs, stingrays, frogs, snakes, snails, jellyfish, clownfish, pufferfish, an octopus, ducks, shrimp and dragonflies.
      • New fish species are invented as you watch, each with its own shape, colours, pattern and name.
      • Animals get hungry, grow up, lay eggs, pass their genes on, hunt, flee and eventually move on.
      • A day/night cycle with fireflies, rain showers, wind, ripples and an optional generative soundscape.
      • Freshwater, saltwater or both: reef fish, corals and starfish, or koi, frogs and lily pads.
      • Genetics with rare albino, melanistic, piebald and shiny animals that breed true.
      • Every pond has a seed: share the link and friends get the same pond.
