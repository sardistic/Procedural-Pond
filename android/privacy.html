Pond for Android: privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026. This policy covers the offline Android app published by sardistic, package nz.pond.game.

The app does not collect or transmit personal information. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, or network permission. The game and its assets are bundled in the app.

Your ponds

Ponds, settings, resident names, ambitions and album memories stay in the app's local storage. They are not sent to us or synchronized with pond.nz. You can delete ponds in the game. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local progress. App backup is disabled.

Photos and external links

When you save a pond photo, Android asks you where to save the image. The app writes only the image you requested to that chosen location. Exported images remain until you delete them. External links open in your browser; those websites follow their own privacy policies. Google Play and Android may separately process information under their own policies.

Contact and changes

For questions, contact the developer through Pond's support page. Do not post private information in a public issue. Any change to this app's data practices will be reflected here with an updated date.
