# Google Play listing draft

App name: **Pond**

Short description (under 80 characters):

Grow a living pixel-art pond. Feed, breed, plant and discover the depths.

Full description:

Start with a small pond and watch a world of life unfold.

Every animal moves through procedural animation. Feed your residents, plant
their favorite habitats, and watch new generations grow. Follow individual
animals, understand their needs, and keep favorite residents and milestones in
your personal album.

Choose fresh water, salt water or a mixture. Build shelters and nurseries, shape
the habitat, and discover stranger life as your pond wears deeper. Optional care
guidance and personal ambitions give you ideas for what to try next, while you
set your own pace.

- Living ecosystems with feeding, breeding, hunting and migration.
- Procedurally animated pixel-art creatures and changing water and light.
- Plants, structures, discoveries and increasingly unusual depths.
- Resident favorites, memories and optional care ambitions.
- Portrait and landscape play, adjustable graphics and pond photo export.
- Offline play with local saves. No account, ads or in-app purchases.

This Android edition keeps your ponds on this device. Clearing app storage or
uninstalling removes progress. It does not sync with pond.nz; website visiting,
rankings and online minds are not part of this edition.

Suggested category: Simulation (Game).
